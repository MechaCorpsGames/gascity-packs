# Remove secrets from current memory

A secret is removed through the owning City Life operation from current fields that accept user content through supported `remember` or `revise` operations and from rig-relevance metadata. City Life's diagnostics identify this write boundary and direct the operator to Beads for any other current Memory field; they do not claim to detect secrets outside the pack's surface. Archive is not redaction: archived content remains recallable and restorable. City Life does not filter required upstream identity, History, or Change Attribution and makes no cleanup claim about retained versions, provider conversations, exports, replicas, or backups.
