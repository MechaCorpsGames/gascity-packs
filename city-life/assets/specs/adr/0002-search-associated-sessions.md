# Search associated sessions

Session search covers every still-existing native conversation associated with the citizen across the city and its rigs. Provider-deleted conversations are absent rather than archived by City Life. Results carry enough provenance to identify their native conversation and report honest continuation or completeness. City scope selects city-scoped conversations; rig scopes add the selected rig; all scope adds every rig. Scope changes relevance only and never ownership.
