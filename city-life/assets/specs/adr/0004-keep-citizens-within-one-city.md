# Keep each citizen within one city

A citizen belongs to one Gas City city and may work at city scope or in any of that city's rigs through any supported harness. Gas City's binding-qualified source-agent name is the identity; Gas City remains authoritative for the citizen's name, role, prompt, and skills. Rig and worktree instances do not create new citizens. The same name in another city identifies a different citizen, so memory, sessions, and identity never cross the city boundary.
