# Agentic memory landscape

**Snapshot:** 2026-08-16/17. Nineteen of twenty-five challenged claims survived verification.

## Observed

- claude-mem uses a layered retrieval flow: compact search records, a timeline, then full observations. Its session-start briefing is token-budgeted.
- claude-mem's capture path is coupled to Claude Code hooks and automatic observation extraction. Local write parity across other coding-agent harnesses was not established.
- Mem0 and Zep/Graphiti also center automatic extraction and model-selected consolidation. Their benchmark claims were not independently decisive in this research.
- None of the reviewed products supplied the combination City Life needs: Gas City source-agent identity, deliberate Memory writes, project-qualified references, and provider-neutral operation.

## Implications for City Life

- Progressive disclosure is the useful precedent: compact discovery first, complete body only after exact selection.
- Automatically extracted observations may become suggestions or search aids, but they must not silently become accepted Memory state.
- Semantic or graph ranking may sit behind the City Life memory module only as an advertised retrieval enhancement. Its absence or degradation must remain visible.
- Canonical identity, archive state, references, guarded writes, and structured records stay in Memory Beads rather than a retrieval sidecar.
