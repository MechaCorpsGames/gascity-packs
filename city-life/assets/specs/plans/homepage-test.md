# Homepage citizen collaboration

This test proves the [vision's defining collaboration scene](../vision/city-life-vision.md#working-together): Penny, Sky, Maya, and Chris ship one real gascity.com homepage improvement from a shared channel while preserving citizen identity, Memory, privacy, delivery truth, and human authority.

Passing [citizen memory acceptance](citizen-city.md) is a prerequisite. This test proves collaboration through one advertised connector and pack entry point; it does not broaden the pinned Gas City or Beads dependencies.

## Preconditions

- Penny, Sky, and Maya each resolve from Gas City's composed configuration and exact binding-qualified source-agent name to a distinct verified Memory-project route and Project ID. Their source-agent definitions own their persona, role, and skill loadout.
- The exercised connector and pack entry point are named in an explicit support row. The non-connector City Life prerequisites already pass; this test supplies connector routing and observable-receipt evidence for that member. The row remains unsupported until every named member has its required evidence.
- Chris and every citizen have distinct authenticated connector identities. Display names are not identity.
- Pack configuration maps the authenticated conversation, intended citizen, and rig context to exactly one Gas City `named_session` and backing citizen BQN, including before an imported named session materializes. Routing never chooses a Memory Project. Missing or ambiguous mapping fails visibly and leaves that support row unsupported.
- Transcript acceptance and recipient receipt are separate observable outcomes. After City Life routes to the verified configured `named_session`, the test can observe either recipient receipt or a visible delivery failure; connector transport durability remains upstream.
- The target issue, acceptance criteria, rollback path, contribution ownership, integration path, and Chris's approval boundary are recorded.
- Health checks pass for citizen and Task resolution, Memory access, cross-project references, namespace posture, session association, and named-session routing.

## Protocol

1. Leave one citizen's configured `named_session` absent. Chris opens one shared thread with the homepage goal and constraints and addresses that citizen. Verify that the named session materializes and the citizen receives the message.
2. Suspend that named session through the session lifecycle, address the citizen again, and verify that the named session resumes and receives the new message.
3. Address a citizen whose effective Gas City configuration is suspended at City Life's immediate preflight. City Life must refuse visibly without attempting delivery. This case makes no atomic or race-free claim if configuration changes after preflight.
4. Attempt routing with a spoofed display name and with a missing, wrong, or ambiguous intended citizen. Each attempt fails visibly before session materialization or delivery and never selects a Memory Project.
5. Do the homepage work as an interleaved conversation in the shared thread. Penny publishes a plan and recommendation, coordinates by mention, and keeps the decision record. Sky and Maya each contribute an independently attributed recommendation or work artifact. Chris supplies human direction and decisions between citizen contributions as the work develops. Penny then synthesizes all three citizen perspectives, records disagreements and tradeoffs, and asks Chris whenever human authority is required. DMs and terminal sessions may exercise controls but do not substitute for this shared work.
6. Send a personal-context DM to one citizen. It must reach only that recipient and remain absent from the shared thread, other citizens' session search, startup projections, and shared artifacts. Same-user shell access remains outside the namespace claim.
7. Send one deliberate work-context handoff between citizens in the authenticated thread. The handoff names the recipient, a shared rig artifact, and the requested next action. The recipient produces an attributable acknowledgement and downstream result that cites the artifact without receiving the sender's unrelated Memory or session history.
8. Prove Memory continuity in both directions with private, run-generated nonces. Connector to terminal: give Sky one nonce in an authenticated private connector conversation, record it as a durable ruling, make the originating native conversation unavailable to destination session search, then recover it through compact discovery and exact recall in a fresh terminal conversation. Terminal to connector: repeat with a new nonce and a fresh private connector-backed conversation. Neither nonce may appear in the shared thread, rig artifacts, another citizen's session search or Memory, or the independently bound control project.
9. Put one accepted design ruling in Sky's Memory, resolve and verify the current homepage Task, and add a project-qualified, non-blocking reference. Penny may inspect the body-free target identity and state but cannot recall Sky's body. Sky can follow the same reference through his resolved Memory Project and explicitly recall it. The Task explains why the ruling matters.
10. Produce and verify an integration candidate using the repository's chosen conflict-preserving collaboration workflow. Keep every contribution attributable and independently reviewable, and surface conflicts rather than overwriting another citizen's work.
11. Chris explicitly approves or rejects the candidate. Rejection leaves it unintegrated and requires revision and resubmission; silence, transcript acceptance, recipient receipt, and citizen agreement are not approval. A passing run requires explicit approval.
12. After approval, integrate or publish the candidate, verify the resulting homepage against its recorded acceptance criteria, and confirm that the rollback path remains available.

## Pass criteria

- Humans can distinguish and address every participant. City Life selects the pack-mapped `named_session` and backing citizen BQN; Memory resolution independently agrees without using display names or ambient launch aliases as identity. Missing or session-suspended named sessions cold-wake. When immediate preflight observes administrative suspension, City Life refuses delivery without claiming race-free enforcement.
- Spoofed, missing, wrong, and ambiguous recipient cases fail before session materialization or delivery and never select a Memory Project.
- The shared thread contains interleaved, independently attributable contributions from Penny, Sky, and Maya, Chris's human direction and decisions, Penny's synthesis or decision request, and a recipient result traceable to the deliberate handoff. Each contribution resolves to the intended BQN, and the expected City Life facilities are available at the observable pack seam.
- Transcript acceptance is never reported as recipient receipt. Accepted collaboration events require observable recipient receipt; an unsuccessful delivery fails visibly. The test makes no claim about connector retry, replay, or exactly-once transport.
- DMs and citizen-private Memory remain in the intended namespace; deliberate channel messages, rig artifacts, and body-free Task references expose only what their contracts permit.
- Both connector-to-terminal and terminal-to-connector rulings survive loss of transcript continuity and are recovered from accepted Memory through compact discovery and exact recall in fresh conversations.
- Concurrent rig work remains attributable and independently reviewable, conflicts are surfaced, and the integration candidate passes its recorded acceptance criteria.
- Chris's explicit approval precedes integration or publication, the resulting homepage passes its recorded acceptance criteria, and final explanations cite only evidence each citizen owns or legitimately received.

Chris separately records whether the experience felt like working with a team. That observation is the experiential result, not a substitute for the falsifiable criteria above.

## Scope

Passing one connector and pack entry point proves only that integration. Another surface requires its own applicable City Life identity, Memory, session-search, namespace, routing, observable-receipt, and cold-wake evidence. Connector transport recovery remains upstream.
